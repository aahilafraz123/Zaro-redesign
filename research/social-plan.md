# Social layer: Journal + Dr. Rena Malik

## Why
Zaro's homepage sells a product. People don't buy blood tests on a first visit; they buy once
they trust the source and understand why a marker matters to them. Two things build that:
**education** (the Journal) and **a trusted human voice** (Dr. Rena Malik). Both also give people
something to share, which a pricing page never does.

## Funnel
1. Discover: Dr. Malik's audience (3M followers, 550M+ YouTube views) and search traffic to
   Journal articles ("what is Lp(a)", "how often should you get blood work").
2. Learn: Journal article or her partner page explains one problem in plain language.
3. Connect: every article and every topic on her page points to the exact panel that measures it
   ("Measured in Signal"). Content always ends one click from checkout.
4. Convert: Signal is the natural landing panel (hormones, Lp(a), NMR) for both audiences.
5. Return: community links (r/ZaroHealth, Instagram, X, LinkedIn) and new articles.

## Pages
- `/blog` (Zaro Journal): its own page, not a homepage section. Featured article, topic filters,
  "Measured in" panel tags, a "Watch with Dr. Malik" video row, community links, panel CTA.
  Articles open the originals on zarohealth.com until the CMS is wired in.
- `/dr-rena-malik`: partner page. Official headshot, verified credentials and reach, "What she
  talks about, what Zaro measures" (men/women hormone tabs mapped to Signal markers), three of her
  testosterone videos, her channels, Signal CTA.
- Homepage: a partner band before Panels (expert voice right before price), a "From the journal"
  row before the FAQ, nav links to both pages.

## Guardrails
- No invented quotes, endorsements or claims about what Dr. Malik recommends. The page states the
  partnership and her public, verifiable background only. Swap in an approved quote and video once
  her team provides them.
- Headshot is her official image (named in her site's structured data), referenced from her site,
  not re-hosted. Replace with a partnership-approved asset before launch.
- The partnership is not public yet (no search results). Do not deploy publicly until her team
  approves the announcement.
